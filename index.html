We believe informed customers make the best decisions.

Do you handle repairs as well as replacements?

Yes. Reynolds Roofing specializes in roof repair, replacement, inspections and storm damage restoration.

Do you serve commercial properties?

Reynolds Roofing serves residential and light commercial customers in its local service area.

What areas do you serve?

The company is based near Wayne, New Jersey, and serves customers within approximately 15 miles.

What happens after a new roof is installed?

Reynolds Roofing is known for minor incidental repair and maintenance support for up to ten years following installation, often without additional charge. Ask the team for details about coverage and eligibility.
